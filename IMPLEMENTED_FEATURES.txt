=====================================================
            CAMPUS EVENT HUB - NEW FEATURES GUIDE
=====================================================

This document outlines the major features recently implemented for 
Admit Card and Certificate management.

-----------------------------------------------------
1. ADMIT CARD SYSTEM
-----------------------------------------------------
- AUTOMATIC GENERATION: A unique 6-digit alphanumeric 'Admit Card Code' 
  is generated as soon as a registration is APPROVED by an admin.
- PDF DOWNLOAD: Students can download their Admit Card directly from 
  their Dashboard once approved.
- LAYOUT: Professional A4 PDF containing Student Details, Event Title, 
  Venue, Date, and a dashed box with the Verification Code.

-----------------------------------------------------
2. ATTENDANCE & CERTIFICATE ISSUANCE (ADMIN)
-----------------------------------------------------
- ATTENDANCE REQUIREMENT: Certificates can ONLY be issued to students 
  marked as 'Present' (Attendance marked by the host admin).
- ISSUANCE FLOW: 
  1. Go to Registration Management.
  2. Mark student as 'Present'.
  3. Click 'Issue Certificate'.
- NOTIFICATION: Upon issuance, the student is automatically notified 
  via email (and a dashboard toast upon their next login).

-----------------------------------------------------
3. CERTIFICATE DOWNLOAD (STUDENT)
-----------------------------------------------------
- DASHBOARD BUTTON: A new 'Certificate' button appears on the student's 
  registration card once the admin issues it.
- LANDSCAPE DESIGN: Downloads a high-quality landscape PDF with elegant 
  serif typography (resembling the email theme).
- SMART ALIGNMENT: The template uses dynamic spacing to ensure text 
  never overlaps, even with long Event Titles or College names.

-----------------------------------------------------
4. NOTIFICATION & LOGGING SYSTEM
-----------------------------------------------------
- LOGIN TOASTS: Students see specific toast notifications when they log 
  in ("Congratulations! Your certificate has been issued!") with a 
  graduation cap icon (🎓).
- EMAIL PREVIEWS: In development mode, check the BACKEND TERMINAL 
  system logs for 'ETHEREAL PREVIEW URLs'. You can click these to see 
  exactly what the student receives.
- ACTIVITY FEED: The 'Activities' section on the dashboard now correctly 
  shows "Certificate issued for [Event]" updates.

-----------------------------------------------------
HOW TO TEST THE FULL FLOW:
-----------------------------------------------------
1. LOGIN AS ADMIN: Approve a pending registration.
2. CHECK TERMINAL: Look for "🎫 Admit Card Preview URL" to see the email.
3. LOGIN AS STUDENT: Download the Admit Card from the Dashboard.
4. LOGIN AS ADMIN: Mark the student as 'Present' and click 'Issue Certificate'.
5. LOGIN AS STUDENT: 
   - Observe the "Congratulations! Your certificate..." toast.
   - Click the new 'Certificate' button to download the PDF.
